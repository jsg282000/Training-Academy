Training Standards

How we teach aesthetic medicine responsibly

Our programmes follow widely accepted principles for physician-led aesthetic education. JSG Academy is an independent academy and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or accredited by any external aesthetic medicine body.

Physician-led teaching

Clinical content is taught by a practising registered physician. Faculty qualifications are published for every programme.

Verified eligibility

Hands-on places are limited to registered medical practitioners. We check your registration before you enrol.

Safety in every module

Each procedural module covers patient selection, contraindications, consent, infection prevention and complication management.

Assessed, not just attended

Online knowledge checks, plus a faculty-observed skills checklist in person. Your certificate records what you completed and were assessed on.

Supervised small groups

Hands-on sessions run under direct physician supervision. The faculty-to-participant ratio is stated in writing before you pay.

Clear about recognition

You receive a certificate of completion only. It is not a licence, and it does not widen your scope of practice. Check your scope with your medical council.

JSG Academy · Academic Programme

Training Curriculum

A structured learning pathway covering aesthetic skin science, theoretical principles, doctor-led demonstrations, clinical safety awareness and supervised practical education for eligible healthcare professionals.

Programme format

Online theory, online demonstrations and in-person practical workshops are separate learning components. Clinical modules are intended for registered medical practitioners. Availability, faculty, duration, eligibility, assessment method and fees are confirmed for each programme.

01
ONLINE THEORY

Foundations of Skin Science

  • Structure and function of the skin
  • Epidermis, dermis and skin appendages
  • Skin barrier, hydration and pigmentation
  • Skin types and common cosmetic concerns
  • Intrinsic and extrinsic skin ageing
  • Sun exposure and environmental influences

Learning outcome: Understand foundational skin anatomy, physiology and common cosmetic skin-care terminology.

02
ONLINE THEORY

Cosmetic Ingredients and Skin Care

  • Moisturisers, cleansers and sunscreens
  • Common cosmetic ingredient categories
  • Product selection and label interpretation
  • Evidence-based evaluation of cosmetic claims
  • Skin-care routines and realistic expectations
  • Recognising concerns that require medical assessment

Learning outcome: Develop informed understanding of cosmetic products and their appropriate use.

03
ONLINE THEORY

Aesthetic Assessment and Consultation Principles

  • Understanding client expectations
  • Relevant history-taking concepts
  • Skin concerns and assessment terminology
  • Patient selection principles for qualified clinicians
  • Contraindications and referral considerations
  • Informed consent, documentation and confidentiality

Learning outcome: Understand consultation principles, professional boundaries and responsible decision-making.

04
ONLINE THEORY + DEMONSTRATIONS

Aesthetic Treatment Principles

  • Overview of non-invasive skin-care approaches
  • Introduction to chemical exfoliation concepts
  • Energy-based devices: principles and safety awareness
  • Overview of microneedling and other clinical modalities
  • Understanding treatment limitations and risks
  • Patient safety and appropriate referral

Learning outcome: Understand treatment categories, general principles and the importance of appropriate clinical training.

Educational demonstrations do not confer independent competence or authorisation to perform procedures.

05
ONLINE THEORY + CASE REVIEW

Clinical Anatomy, Safety and Complication Management

  • Facial and skin anatomy relevant to aesthetic procedures
  • Anatomical danger zones and how to avoid them
  • Pharmacology and product safety basics
  • Recognising early and late adverse events
  • Emergency preparedness and escalation protocols
  • Reviewing complications through anonymised cases

Learning outcome: Recognise risks early and know when and how to escalate or refer.

06
ONLINE PRACTICAL LEARNING

Doctor-led Demonstrations and Case Discussions

  • Recorded or live educational demonstrations
  • Clinical case discussions using appropriately anonymised cases
  • Consultation and documentation scenarios
  • Discussion of treatment-planning principles
  • Recognition of adverse events and escalation pathways
  • Question-and-answer sessions with faculty

Learning outcome: Observe decision-making and safety principles in an educational setting.

07
IN-PERSON · SUBJECT TO ELIGIBILITY

Supervised Hands-on Practical Workshop

  • Verification of valid medical registration and prerequisites
  • Orientation to the learning environment and safety protocols
  • Equipment familiarisation where relevant and permitted
  • Supervised practice only within approved scope
  • Infection prevention and consent principles
  • Faculty-observed skills checklist and individual feedback
  • Faculty-to-participant ratio confirmed in writing

Learning outcome: Participate in permitted supervised skills learning appropriate to the learner's qualifications and professional scope.

Practical sessions will be offered only when suitable faculty, facilities, approvals and participant eligibility have been confirmed.

08
PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Ethics, Safety and Practice Management

  • Professional boundaries and ethical communication
  • Privacy, documentation and record-keeping concepts
  • Recognising complications and escalation needs
  • Patient experience and service quality
  • Ethical marketing and managing expectations
  • Practice planning and basic performance indicators

Learning outcome: Build a responsible framework for communication, safety awareness and professional development.

09
ASSESSMENT

Competency Check and Certificate of Completion

  • Written knowledge assessment covering all completed modules
  • Case-based questions on safety and referral decisions
  • Skills checklist observed by faculty (hands-on pathway only)
  • Written feedback and areas for further study
  • Certificate stating the modules completed and assessed

Learning outcome: Show that you have met the stated objectives of each module you completed.

The certificate records course completion only. It is not a licence or an accredited qualification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Before you begin

Are all courses available online?

Online theory and online educational demonstrations may be offered remotely. Hands-on workshops, if offered, require an appropriate in-person setting and confirmed eligibility.

Will I be qualified to perform aesthetic procedures after completing a course?

No course should be assumed to grant a licence, registration or expanded professional scope. Eligibility to perform any procedure depends on applicable law, professional registration, qualifications and relevant permissions.

Who can join hands-on training?

Clinical practical sessions are limited to registered medical practitioners whose scope permits the relevant activity. We check your registration before enrolment.

How is my learning assessed?

Online modules include knowledge checks and a final written assessment. The hands-on workshop adds a faculty-observed skills checklist with individual feedback. Your certificate lists what you completed and were assessed on.

Is JSG Academy affiliated with the American Academy of Aesthetic Medicine?

No. JSG Academy is independent. We build our curriculum around widely accepted principles of physician-led, safety-first training, but we do not claim affiliation, endorsement or accreditation from any external body.

Are these courses accredited?

No accreditation or formal recognition is claimed on this page. Ask the academy for written confirmation of any specific recognition before enrolling.

How do I pay the course fee?

Contact the academy to receive confirmed course details, final fee, tax information, cancellation/refund terms and the approved payment method. This website does not currently process payments.

What is the refund policy?

The applicable cancellation and refund policy must be supplied in writing before payment. Please request and review it before enrolling.
